# Put your real photo in the portfolio

## Option A — fastest (no code)
1. Open the site and click **"pin your photo"** under the portrait in the hero.
2. Choose your portrait — it instantly appears in the hero and about frames.
   It is resized, stored in your browser only, and remembered on that device.

## Option B — publish it for everyone
Save your portrait in this folder as **shruti.jpg** and rebuild.
(`profile.jpg`, `shruti.png`, `profile.png` and `/images/shruti.jpg` also work.)

## Option C — use an image URL
Set `photoUrl` in `src/data.ts` → `PROFILE` to a direct https image link.

The desk photos (night setup) live in `src/assets/setup-night.jpg` and
`src/assets/setup-closeup.jpg` — replace those two files to swap the setup shots.
